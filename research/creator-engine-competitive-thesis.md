# Galinyx Creator Engine — Competitive Thesis

Date: 2026-10-14. Status: unproven concept, not first-of-kind.

## Competitors
- Higgsfield AI Influencer Studio: https://higgsfield.ai/blog/new-ai-influencer
- Higgsfield Earn paid creator campaigns: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/business/how-do-i-join-a-partnership-or-creator-program
- Palette, YC S26, autonomous media creation, distribution and optimization: https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/palette-2
- Voplica: character continuity, autonomous agents, proposed AI social network: https://voplica.com/

## Differentiation hypothesis — not yet validated
Rights-aware multi-character entertainment operating system with persistent fictional canon, cross-media continuity, attributable gross profit, and a full production-cost ledger. Do not claim uniqueness before feature-by-feature validation.

## Architecture
1. Character registry: visual/voice identity, boundaries, provenance, disclosure.
2. Storyworld graph: canonical relationships, events, timeline, continuity.
3. Production pipeline: briefs, generation adapters, editorial approval, cost tracking.
4. Rights gate: likeness consent, asset license, music rights, synthetic disclosure.
5. Distribution: supported official APIs only; human-controlled accounts.
6. Revenue: affiliate attribution, sponsorship contracts, receipts, expenses and margin.
7. Learning: retention, conversions, production cost and contribution margin, not views alone.

## Falsifiable first test
Create one original character, ten consistent assets, four-week story arc, rights record and cost report. Blind-review identity consistency; compare costs and human time with a manual baseline. No automatic posting or payout promises in v0.

## Go/no-go
- At least 90% of test assets pass a predefined blinded identity rubric.
- Every published asset has provenance, license review and required disclosures.
- Production cost and review time per asset are recorded.
- Demonstrate authentic audience engagement and an attributable monetization event before scaling.
- Stop or pivot if costs, platform policies or demand invalidate the thesis.

## Build order
Data model -> offline character/storyworld generator -> approval workflow -> cost ledger -> content adapters -> distribution and revenue integrations after policy checks.
