# GALINYX Ecosystem & NYX AI Division Charter

**Status:** Adopted product/brand architecture decision  
**Decision:** NYX AI is an integrated division of the GALINYX ecosystem. NYX AI retains its own name, identity, audience, and mission.

## 1. Governing principle

**One ecosystem. Distinct divisions. Shared infrastructure. Independent identities.**

GALINYX is the master ecosystem. NYX AI is not being retired, renamed, or absorbed into the GALINYX name. It remains a recognizable division with a distinct purpose and audience.

This is an organizational and product-brand architecture decision. It does not, by itself, establish a separate legal entity, subsidiary, ownership structure, or trademark status.

## 2. Brand and product hierarchy

- **GALINYX** — master brand and overall AI ecosystem.
- **GALINYX OS** — foundational operating platform and environment for building and operating governed AI systems.
- **Governance Core** — native authority, policy, permission, approval, and accountability layer within GALINYX OS. It is not a separate platform.
- **GALINYX AI Workforce** — agent teams and orchestration capabilities operating within authorized environments.
- **NYX AI** — integrated education, training, creator-development, and community division of GALINYX.
- **NYX AI Academy** — learning and training program within the NYX AI division.
- **NYX AI Creator Network** — proposed future community and creator-development program; not represented as shipped.
- **GALINYX Marketplace** — proposed future discovery, distribution, and monetization layer for agents and applications; not represented as shipped.

## 3. NYX AI mission and boundaries

NYX AI helps people understand, learn, create, evaluate, and responsibly develop AI capabilities.

Its potential responsibilities include:
- AI literacy and practical education.
- Structured learning paths and developer onboarding.
- Training on GALINYX OS and its Governance Core.
- Agent-building exercises and evaluation.
- Creator community, collaboration, and showcases.
- Preparation for publishing or monetizing AI products through future GALINYX ecosystem services.

NYX AI should retain its own visual identity, voice, learning experience, and audience relationships while clearly identifying itself as part of GALINYX.

## 4. Shared infrastructure

Where appropriate, NYX AI may use shared GALINYX services:
- Identity and account infrastructure.
- Workspace and organization management.
- Governance policies and authorization.
- AI model and agent runtime services.
- Knowledge, document, and evidence services.
- Integrations and developer tooling.
- Observability, security, and audit services.
- Billing and marketplace infrastructure when implemented.

Shared infrastructure does not mean unrestricted data sharing. Learner, creator, developer, and business workspace data must remain appropriately isolated, permissioned, and auditable.

## 5. Relationship to GALINYX OS

NYX AI is a division of the broader GALINYX ecosystem, not a replacement for GALINYX OS and not the name of the operating platform.

NYX AI can teach users to build on GALINYX OS. GALINYX OS provides the platform capabilities; the Governance Core defines and enforces authority boundaries; NYX AI provides education, training, and creator development.

## 6. Developer and creator journey

The intended future journey is:
1. Discover GALINYX and NYX AI.
2. Learn through NYX AI Academy.
3. Create an account and workspace in GALINYX OS.
4. Build or configure agents using reusable platform capabilities and governance presets.
5. Test behavior, evidence handling, and permission boundaries.
6. Publish or deploy through authorized GALINYX services when available.
7. Participate in the wider creator ecosystem and potential marketplace.

These are architectural goals, not claims that every service is currently implemented.

## 7. Product principles

- Preserve NYX AI's identity; do not collapse all naming into GALINYX.
- Keep GALINYX OS as the platform name.
- Keep Governance Core native to GALINYX OS.
- Separate education and community functions from platform execution and enforcement.
- Use shared services without compromising tenant or workspace isolation.
- Make product capabilities, certification claims, and monetization availability explicit and verifiable.
- Do not describe proposed divisions or future capabilities as live products until implemented and tested.

## 8. Implementation implications

The repository and product roadmap should reflect this hierarchy. The next practical steps are to inventory existing NYX AI assets and routes, map them to the NYX AI division, define the cross-brand navigation and identity system, and then implement shared account/workspace and platform connections without prematurely merging or deleting existing NYX AI materials.

## 9. Decision record

The product direction is confirmed: **NYX AI is an integrated division of GALINYX, retaining its own identity.** Future changes to this relationship should be treated as explicit architecture decisions rather than accidental naming changes.
