# GALINYX Intellectual Property Ownership & Protection Standard

**Status:** Internal ownership and handling directive  
**Effective date:** 2026-11-16  
**Intended owner:** The founder personally, as an individual  
**Scope:** GALINYX, GALINYX OS, Governance Core, AI Workforce, Control Room, NYX AI, NYX AI Academy, and original related software, documentation, designs, methods, policies, workflows, and technical materials.

> **Important legal status:** This document records the founder's intended ownership and internal handling rules. It is not a legal opinion, copyright registration, patent filing, trademark registration, assignment, or proof that every item listed is legally protectable or exclusively owned. Actual ownership can depend on authorship, employment or contractor terms, third-party rights, applicable law, and the nature of AI-assisted contributions. Obtain qualified IP counsel before relying on legal conclusions or making filings.

## 1. Ownership direction — no transfer

The founder's stated intention is to own GALINYX intellectual property personally.

- No intellectual property is assigned, transferred, contributed, sold, licensed, pledged, or otherwise conveyed by this document.
- No ownership is transferred to Southeast Home Service, BLACKPINE FIELD, NYX AI as a separate entity, or any existing or future company.
- No company or holding company is represented as already formed or as the current owner.
- Any future company formation, contribution, assignment, license, or restructuring requires a separate written instrument and the founder's express approval, with legal review.
- Project workspaces and operating environments do not acquire ownership merely because they use GALINYX technology.

## 2. Covered work and asset register

Treat the following as potentially proprietary GALINYX assets pending item-by-item rights review:

- GALINYX and GALINYX OS product architecture and original implementation.
- Governance Core: authority models, policy evaluation, permission boundaries, approval concepts, accountability and audit designs, and original code.
- AI Workforce: specialist role definitions, orchestration, evidence controls, validation, handoff formats, and original code.

- NYX AI and NYX AI Academy original curricula, learning paths, training materials, designs, and software.

Maintain an asset register recording each material asset's name, description, creator/contributors, creation date, repository and commit, third-party dependencies, disclosure history, and protection status. Do not assume every idea, method, name, or AI-generated output is protected by copyright.

## 3. Default handling rules

Unless the founder gives specific written authorization:

1. Keep source repositories private and restrict access to people who need it.
2. Do not publish source code, internal architecture, security-sensitive details, proprietary prompts, policy logic, or unreleased documentation.
3. Do not open-source, sublicense, sell, assign, pledge, transfer, or license GALINYX materials.
4. Do not copy GALINYX assets into another business or workspace in a way that changes ownership, control, access, or permitted use.
5. Do not expose credentials, tokens, private keys, customer information, or confidential operational data in source control.
6. Require review of third-party code, datasets, fonts, models, and other dependencies before incorporating or distributing them.
7. Preserve commit history, dated design records, source files, decision records, and backups.
8. Keep a record of every authorized external disclosure, recipient, date, purpose, and confidentiality terms.

A private repository is one access-control measure; it is not a substitute for contracts, backups, security controls, registrations, or legal review.

## 4. Protection workstreams

### Copyright and authorship evidence
- Preserve dated source files, design drafts, commit history, and meaningful human-authorship records.
- Identify human authors and contributors accurately.
- Review contractor, collaborator, and AI-assisted contributions for rights and license conditions.
- Ask IP counsel which original works should be registered and who should be named as claimant/author under applicable law.
- Use a reviewed copyright notice where appropriate; do not use a notice to imply rights that have not been established.

### Trade secrets and confidentiality
- Identify information whose value depends on remaining confidential.
- Limit access, use least-privilege permissions, enable account security protections, and keep secrets outside repositories.
- Use appropriate confidentiality and invention/IP agreements before sharing confidential material with contractors, advisors, or partners.
- Mark sensitive internal materials as confidential and keep disclosure records.
- Do not publicly disclose potentially patentable inventions before counsel assesses filing strategy.

### Patents
- Maintain dated invention disclosures describing the technical problem, novel mechanism, alternatives, contributors, and disclosure history.
- Have a qualified patent professional assess novelty, inventorship, eligibility, prior art, filing costs, and jurisdictions.
- Do not assume software, business methods, AI workflows, or governance concepts are patentable merely because they are original or useful.

### Trademarks
- Treat GALINYX, GALINYX OS, NYX AI, NYX AI Academy, and related marks as proposed brand assets pending clearance.
- Before significant public brand investment, obtain a trademark clearance review and assess appropriate goods/services and filing jurisdictions.
- Do not use ® unless the relevant mark is registered for the relevant goods/services. ™ may be used as a claim of trademark use, subject to legal review.

### Contracts and third-party rights
- Before outside development or collaboration, use counsel-reviewed agreements addressing confidentiality, background IP, newly created IP, licenses, deliverables, and permitted reuse.
- Track open-source and commercial dependency licenses and comply with their terms.
- Do not claim exclusive ownership of third-party, public-domain, or AI-generated material without a valid legal basis.

## 5. Repository and release controls

- Keep the GALINYX source repository private by default.
- Review collaborator access periodically and remove access when no longer required.
- Protect important branches and require review before changes reach the primary branch where repository settings permit.
- Preserve independent backups and verify restoration periodically.
- Scan for accidentally committed secrets and rotate any exposed credential immediately.
- Keep releases and public demonstrations limited to approved materials; use sanitized examples rather than confidential implementation details.
- Do not merge, deploy, publish, or make a repository public solely because automated checks pass.

## 6. Business and workspace separation

GALINYX is the white-collar technology and IP lane. Southeast Home Service and construction/trades operations remain a separate blue-collar lane. BLACKPINE FIELD is an operating workspace and test environment, not the owner of GALINYX IP. Shared use requires defined scope and permission; it does not imply a transfer of ownership, data, customers, money, or authority.

## 7. Approval rule

The founder's explicit approval is required before any of the following:
- Making a GALINYX repository or proprietary source public.
- Applying an open-source or other public license.
- Assigning, selling, contributing, pledging, or transferring IP.
- Granting a third party a license or commercial reuse right.
- Disclosing confidential implementation details or a potentially patentable invention.
- Moving GALINYX assets into another legal entity or business.

A technical merge, deployment, integration, or use by a workspace is not an IP transfer and must not be described as one.
