# Blackpine Field — Initial Digital Operating Backlog

**Sprint objective:** Establish a verified operating picture and identify the shortest credible path from existing digital assets to measurable commerce.

## Priority 0 — establish facts
- [ ] Confirm canonical storefront URL and current availability.
- [ ] Inventory product listings, prices, descriptions, shipping/fulfillment statements, policies, and calls to action.
- [ ] Inventory published videos and their destination links.
- [ ] Reconcile the five reported Awin applications: advertiser, submission date, status, next action, and evidence.
- [ ] Inspect the Blackpine GitHub source-of-truth repository and compare committed assets with reported assets.
- [ ] Record analytics sources and the date range available; do not assume tracking is installed.

**Acceptance:** A dated baseline report with source links/evidence and explicit unknowns.

## Priority 1 — commercial readiness
- [ ] Identify the primary customer segment and the customer problem each current offer addresses.
- [ ] Review product-page clarity: who it is for, what is included, limitations, proof, price, and next step.
- [ ] Check consistency between storefront claims, video claims, affiliate disclosures, and actual product/fulfillment capability.
- [ ] Rank the top three conversion or trust improvements by impact and effort.
- [ ] Draft improved copy and page structure for human review; do not publish without approval.

**Acceptance:** Prioritized recommendations with before/after drafts and rationale.

## Priority 2 — distribution and partnerships
- [ ] Map each existing video to a specific audience, offer, and trackable destination.
- [ ] Prepare a two-week organic content calendar using existing assets.
- [ ] Draft (do not send) follow-ups for affiliate applications that need action.
- [ ] Identify additional relevant partner opportunities only after current applications and offer fit are understood.

**Acceptance:** Content calendar, draft follow-ups, and partner pipeline with evidence.

## Priority 3 — measurement
- [ ] Define a minimum weekly scorecard: sessions, product-page views, outbound clicks, orders, revenue, refunds, affiliate clicks/commissions, and content-attributed visits where available.
- [ ] Identify missing tracking and propose a low-cost implementation.
- [ ] Establish a weekly review: what moved, what did not, why, and what decision is needed.

**Acceptance:** Scorecard template with data sources and missing-data flags.

## Explicit exclusions
- No paid advertising or paid software commitments without approval.
- No publishing, external outreach, affiliate acceptance, contract acceptance, or pricing/policy changes without approval.
- No unsupported revenue forecasts or claims of guaranteed traffic/sales.
- No physical fulfillment assumptions.
