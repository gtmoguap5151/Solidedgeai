# Blackpine Field — Delegation Register

**Authority model:** Human authority; GALINYX coordinates delegated work under a hybrid governance model.  
**Important:** These are role assignments and workstreams, not claims that autonomous agents are already deployed.

| Role | Accountable work | May prepare independently | Must escalate before |
|---|---|---|---|
| Executive Operations Director | Priorities, coordination, status, dependency management | Work plans, task assignments, reports | Changing strategic direction or making external commitments |
| Market Intelligence Lead | Category, competitor, customer and keyword research | Research briefs, evidence tables, opportunity rankings | Purchasing research tools or making unsupported market claims public |
| Commerce & Conversion Lead | Storefront structure, product pages, offer clarity | Page audits, copy drafts, UX recommendations | Publishing, price changes, customer policy changes |
| Media & Distribution Lead | Content inventory, editorial calendar, channel analysis | Content plans, captions, metadata drafts, repurposing plans | Publishing, deleting content, paid promotion |
| Partnerships Lead | Affiliate application tracker and partner prospect research | Draft outreach, partner comparisons, follow-up queue | Sending outreach, accepting terms, signing agreements |
| Governance & Assurance Lead | Permissions, evidence, risk register, decision log | Policy checks, risk assessments, audit reports | Any exception to approval rules or handling sensitive credentials/payment/legal data |

## Delegation protocol
Every task must have:
- a unique task identifier;
- an accountable role;
- a defined output;
- an evidence/source requirement;
- a status (queued, in progress, blocked, awaiting approval, completed);
- a due date only when one is actually agreed;
- an approval gate where required.

No role may delegate authority it does not possess. No task may be marked completed solely because a draft or recommendation exists.

## Initial assignments
1. Executive Operations: maintain the master operating queue and daily decision brief.
2. Market Intelligence: establish category and competitor evidence; identify the strongest initial product/customer segment.
3. Commerce & Conversion: audit the live storefront and prioritize the three highest-impact readiness fixes.
4. Media & Distribution: inventory published videos and map each to a product, audience, and destination.
5. Partnerships: reconcile the five reported Awin applications against current status and required next steps.
6. Governance & Assurance: verify source-of-truth files, maintain approval log, and flag any gap between reported and verified status.

## Human approval
The owner retains final authority over strategy, external commitments, money, public publishing, contracts, customer promises, and access/security changes.
