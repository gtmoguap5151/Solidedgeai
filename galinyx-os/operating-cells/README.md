# GALINYX OS — Operating Cell Registry

This directory contains company-specific operating charters, delegation rules, backlogs, and executive status records.

## Portfolio model

GALINYX is the governance and orchestration platform. Portfolio companies remain distinct businesses with their own assets, obligations, and decision rights. GALINYX may coordinate digital work only through explicitly delegated authority.

| Portfolio entity | Relationship | Current operating status |
|---|---|---|
| Blackpine Field | First digital operating-cell test | Initial charter and backlog established; live asset audit pending |
| Southeast Home Service | Owner's established business | Not selected for this first cell because its current work has physical/on-site dependencies |
| Rivet Reach | Product/brand associated with Southeast Home Service | Portfolio record only; not a separate legal company |
| GALINYX OS | Platform and governance infrastructure | Foundational product under development |

## Operating principles
1. Human authority remains final.
2. Digital work is in scope; physical access is not assumed.
3. Roles and delegations in documents are not proof that autonomous agents are deployed.
4. External actions require explicit approval where they create commitments, public changes, spend, or customer impact.
5. Evidence is required before reporting an action as complete or a metric as fact.
6. Each company retains its own identity, records, accounts, and legal status.
