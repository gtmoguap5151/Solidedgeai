# GALINYX

**Explore the Unknown. Prove the Possible.**

Galinyx is a proposed independent technology brand focused on original software, experimentation, and research across disciplines. It is not limited to construction, contracting, or any particular market.

## Mission
Build useful, ambitious technologies that expand human capability. Test bold hypotheses against evidence, and create products people genuinely choose to use.

## Operating principles
1. Evidence over ego. Change our minds when tests contradict assumptions.
2. Invent rather than imitate. Differentiation must be demonstrable.
3. Earn trust. Protect user data, communicate limitations, and never fabricate results.
4. Ship and measure. Launch focused experiments before expanding scope.
5. Think globally, execute deliberately.

## Brand architecture
- **Galinyx** — proposed parent brand, subject to full trademark and domain clearance.
- **Galinyx Labs** — proposed R&D identity, not a separate legal entity.
- **Solid Edge AI** — existing product and codebase; preserve until a controlled migration is approved.

## First release scope
A responsive, accessible corporate landing page describing the mission and showcasing research/products truthfully. No invented product launches, testimonials, customers, investment claims, or promises.

## Launch safeguards
- Complete confusingly similar trademark searches and domain purchase availability checks.
- Obtain control of domain, DNS, deployment, and social handles before public branding.
- Audit existing Stripe/Supabase checkout and protected downloads separately; do not imply a paid product works before end-to-end testing.
- Review privacy, security, accessibility, and legal entity representation.
- Do not overwrite the production site or remove existing business assets until launch approval.

## Design direction
Dark midnight palette, restrained celestial accents, strong typography, generous spacing, clean product-first hierarchy. Professional rather than decorative. The night is a metaphor for the unknown, not a claim of mythology-derived technology.
