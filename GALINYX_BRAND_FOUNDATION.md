# GALINYX — Brand Foundation (Proposed)

Status: **brand selected; legal, domain, and entity clearance pending.** Do not present Galinyx as an incorporated or trademark-registered entity.

## Purpose
Develop original, useful technology across sectors without tying the parent identity to any one industry or product.

## Philosophy
Explore the unknown. Prove the possible.

- Challenge assumptions and test ideas against evidence.
- Build trustworthy, secure, accessible products.
- Prioritize original technology and demonstrable customer value.
- Be accurate about capabilities, ownership, and results.

## Naming architecture
- **Galinyx**: proposed parent technology brand.
- **Galinyx Labs**: potential research-and-development identity, not a separate legal entity.
- **Solid Edge AI**: existing product/project name; preserve until clearance and migration are approved.

## Prelaunch gates
1. Check exact and confusingly similar trademarks in relevant jurisdictions and classes; obtain qualified legal advice where needed.
2. Verify domain availability through a registrar/registry lookup; do not infer availability from search-engine silence.
3. Check entity names, social handles, and confusingly similar technology brands.
4. Verify ownership and access for domain, GitHub, hosting, Supabase, Stripe, and downloadable assets.
5. Confirm deployment, payment, fulfillment, security, and privacy flows before promising sales.
6. Rebrand production assets only after clearance and explicit go-live decision.

## Product strategy
Select the first product through customer discovery and small measurable experiments, not an assumed industry. Record the problem, target user, differentiated solution, cost to deliver, and measurable traction.

## Engineering principles
Version control, peer-reviewable changes, least-privilege credentials, no secrets in source, automated checks, monitoring, backups, and accessible mobile-first UX.
