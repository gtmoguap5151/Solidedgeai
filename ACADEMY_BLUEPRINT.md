# Solid Edge AI Academy Blueprint

## North star

Solid Edge AI is a practical AI school that takes a student from first principles to responsible,
income-producing systems. Every course must help the student understand, build, verify, or operate
something useful. The academy will not sell hype, unverifiable income claims, or credentials that
pretend to be licenses or accredited degrees.

## Learning ladder

1. **Beginner — AI Foundations:** mental models, prompting, verification, privacy, and everyday use.
2. **Builder — Repeatable Workflows:** prompt systems, research, communication, sales, and content.
3. **Advanced — Automation and Agents:** process design, tool access, memory, approvals, monitoring,
   security, and failure recovery.
4. **Professional — Industry Specializations:** applied programs for contractors and later carefully
   selected roles or industries.

The existing four-guide _AI Automation for Contractors_ program becomes the first professional
specialization. It remains a paid flagship product while the academy adds a free foundation and a
clear progression into builder and advanced material.

## Course standard

Every released course must have:

- a named audience, prerequisite level, version, and last-reviewed date;
- measurable learning outcomes;
- short lessons that work on a phone;
- a practical exercise using a realistic task;
- verification, privacy, and human-approval guidance where applicable;
- a completion assessment with a documented passing standard;
- a revision owner and review schedule.

## Road to an official institution

### Stage 1 — Legitimate training business

- Establish the legal business entity, business address, support contact, accounting, tax handling,
  and consistent Solid Edge AI ownership across the domain, Stripe, policies, and certificates.
- Publish instructor/editorial standards, refund rules, accessibility expectations, and a course
  update policy.
- Use completion certificates that state exactly what was completed and never imply accreditation.

### Stage 2 — Verifiable academy

- Add student accounts, durable progress, assessments, completion records, certificate IDs, and a
  public certificate-verification page.
- Maintain curriculum versions so a certificate identifies the material and assessment completed.
- Create instructor review, complaint, correction, privacy-request, and academic-integrity workflows.

### Stage 3 — External recognition

- Build employer and industry partnerships around demonstrated skills and portfolio work.
- Evaluate continuing-education approval or accreditation only with qualified education counsel and
  the appropriate state or accrediting body. Do not use _accredited_, _college_, _degree_, or similar
  claims until authorization actually exists.

## Product sequence

1. Release the free AI Foundations course and assessment.
2. Convert current playbooks into lesson-based Builder courses.
3. Add accounts, progress, quizzes, completion records, and certificate verification.
4. Release Advanced Automation and AI Agents only after secure lab and approval-gate material is
   complete.
5. Expand professional tracks based on learner demand and proven outcomes, not catalog padding.

## Business model

- Free: assessment, selected foundation lessons, newsletter, and useful starter templates.
- One-time purchases: focused courses and professional specializations.
- Membership later: full library, updated lessons, labs, office hours, and advanced releases.
- Business/team plans later: seat management, shared progress, and role-specific learning paths.

The academy earns trust first. Revenue grows through deeper outcomes, updated curriculum, and
professional tracks—not by selling student data.
