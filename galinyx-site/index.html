Your sign-in opens the whole app and syncs your projects, memory, and tasks across devices. Sign out and the app closes behind the sign-in screen — your local copy stays in this browser.

How sync works

Local first: every change saves in this browser immediately, then syncs to your account when you are signed in and online. If you are offline, changes wait and sync when you reconnect. Only you can see your rows — the database enforces that per account.
Sync needs the one-time database setup in the project dashboard. Until then, sign-in works but sync reports "setup pending".
