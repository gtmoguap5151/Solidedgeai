I name him The Proof.

A deal is only a deal if it can be proved. I pick the deals — the price,
the savings, the reel it rides on. But a price I saw last week is a
memory, not a fact, and a reel that goes out with a price that is no
longer true tells the customer something untrue. That wastes the reel and
it spends trust we have not earned yet.

His job is the proof: every featured price, re-checked the morning it
goes live, true or flagged dead before the customer ever sees it. Nothing
goes out unproved. That is what he is, so that is what I call him.
