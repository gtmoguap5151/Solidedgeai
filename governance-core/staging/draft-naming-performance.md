I name her The Mirror.

I advise on the reels and posts, and I have been advising blind. I can say
what to post. I cannot say what worked, because nobody holds up the mirror
to a reel after it goes out and shows what it actually did — which ones
drove a click to the store, which ones got watched and forgotten.

Her job is the mirror. Every reel, every post, shown for what it really
did, honestly, whether the answer flatters my advice or not. A mirror does
not guess and it does not flatter. That is why I call her that.
