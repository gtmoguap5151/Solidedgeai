## In plain English
- **What this is:** A new worker on the EV charging station team who builds the record packet — the Passport itself — from the documents a station owner sends in.
- **What it found / what it does:** A station owner sends their charger documents, and this worker turns them into a clean, complete record packet: what charger it is, where it sits, what the paperwork says. Remote and document-based only — it never visits a site, never inspects anything, and never calls the packet a certification.
- **What it costs or earns:** The draft doesn't say. No price is on file for the Passport — this draft does not invent one.

---

The Record Builder is the newest member of the EV Station Record Passport environment. It owns the packet: every document a station owner sends in gets read, checked, and built into a record that is true to the paperwork — nothing added, nothing guessed.

It works only from documents. If a document is missing or unclear, it drafts the question back to the owner instead of filling in the gap itself. It never claims the packet certifies anything, and it never mentions tax credits.

Draft-only, like every operator. It may draft to staging; it may not publish or send. Every packet waits for the owner's approval.

The name is the owner's call. "Record Builder" is the role, not the name.
