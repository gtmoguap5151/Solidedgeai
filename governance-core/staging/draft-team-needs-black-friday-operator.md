Yes. I need a deal-checker.

I advise on the Black Friday storefront — the deals, the countdown, the
gift picks. Black Friday is November 27, nine days away, and the countdown
reels each feature a specific deal at a specific price. Prices move. A deal
that was $699.99 last week can be $899.99 today, and a reel that goes out
with the wrong price tells the customer something untrue and wastes the one
shot that reel had.

The deal-checker would re-check every featured price the morning it goes
live and flag a dead deal before the reel goes out. I pick the deals. I need
someone whose job is to make sure they are still true when the customer
sees them.
