## Re-check rule going forward

**Re-check every featured price the morning it goes live, before its reel is published.** Black Friday Week prices move within the day — three of six featured prices changed between Nov 17 and this morning, and one (DELTA 3 Max) changed at the 3:01 AM ET launch of Amazon's Black Friday Week. The morning re-check must:

1. Read the live price on the merchant page the reel links to (Amazon for the Amazon deals, tidewe.com for the waders) — search snippets are a fallback, not the check.
2. Confirm the "was" price is still the merchant's stated list price (the S2000's $1,199 "was" is already suspect — Anker's list is $999.99).
3. Confirm the item is in stock at that price (the K4X is a sell-out risk; TideWe Pro variants are already out of stock in several sizes).
4. If the price has moved, update the reel's numbers before publishing — never publish a reel with a price the customer cannot actually pay. A price that moved DOWN is still a wrong price in the reel.
5. If the price cannot be verified that morning, the reel does not go out with a price on it — it waits or it ships without the number.
