## The design

**The True Pine, full back.** A gnarled black pine standing on a rocky ridgeline — roots gripping the rock, layered mountains behind it, two distant pines, birds in the sky. Drawn true to a real black pine, not a cartoon triangle and not the geometric mark. The tree is the hero; the scene grounds it so it never looks like a tree floating alone.

- **Back prints carry the tree and scene ONLY — no name on the back** (Matt's rule).
- **Fronts keep the locked mark:** symmetric Faceted Pine + BLACKPINE + ROOTS RAN DEEP. Unchanged.
- **Ink rule:** black ink on light garments, bone ink on dark garments. The amber pine stays on the label mark only — never on a product.
- **Caps and mugs** carry the Heritage Badge — the locked mark in badge form (faceted pine + BLACKPINE + ROOTS RAN DEEP inside a double ring). That is the brand mark reshaped to fit a cap and a mug, not a second design.
- The dead tagline is nowhere in this line.

## The honest catches

- **The mockups show the pine running seam to seam. A standard back print does not.** The standard maximum back print on a Gildan 5000 is about 14 inches wide (Gildan decoration spec sheet); an adult L shirt is about 22 inches across the chest. So the biggest standard back print covers roughly two-thirds of the back width — large, and far bigger than the old mockups Matt called too small, but not literally edge to edge. True edge-to-edge needs an all-over-print shirt (a different blank, mostly polyester, sublimation printing) — price not checked and not quoted here. Before listing: check the exact max back-print size on the Gildan 5000 with the US provider, and check the all-over-print option, and bring Matt the real numbers.
- **The tee is two-sided** (see the tee section above) — the $12.83 margin assumes the one-print cost. Real margin is lower until the back-print charge is confirmed.
- **Costs are from Nov 18, 2026.** Printify prices move, and colored garments can cost a little more than white (a third-party breakdown puts the uplift at $0.50–$1.50). Recheck every cost in Printify before listing.
- **Shipping figures are estimates**, carried over from the earlier full-line costing. Stripe fees (~2.9% + $0.30 per sale) are not in any margin above.
- **Black mug cost is not verified.** The white mug is $5.03; the black mug must be checked before it is listed or priced.
- **Nothing is printed until a customer buys.** Printify is print-on-demand; the store holds no stock. The pants are the exception — see above.
