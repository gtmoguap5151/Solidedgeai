## The nine lane rules (never break these)

1. **Buyer-first.** Buyers before properties. 40 buyers on the list — that part is done.
2. **Market the ASSIGNMENT, never the house.** Marketing a property itself in Florida can be a third-degree felony (Fla. Stat. §475.42). No address or photos in buyer outreach — the address goes to a buyer only after they confirm proof of funds + assignment acceptance.
3. **Written only.** Email, text, form. No phone calls, ever.
4. **Proof of funds is UNKNOWN for every buyer.** A buyer without POF is not a buyer. Verify POF + assignment acceptance before sharing an address, contract, or property detail.
5. **Do not re-send the first message to anyone already contacted.** Duplicates burn the list.
6. **Lane:** pre-foreclosure in Florida (judicial, ~4–12 months, owner still holds title). Also TX, AZ, NV. NOT Georgia. Avoid KY (HB 62), PA, NC, OK, IL.
7. **Target:** ~$10,000 assignment fee per deal. **Earnest money — owner's ruling, Nov 20 ~19:42 ET: "We don't have to spend no earnest money."** We do not spend earnest money. The tracker still lists the source as open — the owner's word closes it from our side.
8. **FL attorney / title company reviews the contract before anything is signed.** Packet on file: purchase contract + Exhibit A assignment + Exhibit B seller acknowledgment.
9. **Harlan KY is a separate lane** (delinquent-tax shortlist, 307 parcels) — not part of this Florida system.

## The stages — and the two levels of verification

Lead → Verified → Buyer-matched → Deal sheet sent → Under contract → Assigned → Closed. Dead is a stage too, with a recorded reason, never revived without new facts.

**Property verified is NOT deal verified.** Property verified = county records confirm identity, owner, value. Deal verified = that PLUS the Clerk check confirms the case and liens PLUS the owner agreed a price PLUS a contract is signed. **No deal in the file is deal-verified yet.**
