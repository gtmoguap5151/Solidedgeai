## The honest headline

- **Cheapest own-made item Printify can produce: the Ceramic Ornament at
  $1.81** (production only, before shipping). There is no $1 item on
  Printify. The next cheapest are stickers (from $1.20 each) and the Square
  Keychain ($2.12).
- **Shipping is extra on every item and it is the real cost driver.**
  Examples from Printify's own pages: the $1.81 ornament ships from $9.49
  with that cheapest provider; the $2.12 keychain ships from $7.59; mugs
  ship at roughly $5.50–$7.50. The cheapest providers are often overseas,
  which is why their shipping is high and slow. US providers usually cost a
  little more per item but ship faster and cheaper.
- **Cheapest item once delivery is counted:** a single sticker — about
  $1.20 to print + $3.99 shipping = roughly $5.19 at the customer's door.
- **Margins below are retail price minus production cost only.** Shipping,
  payment fees, and the $0.40-per-order holiday surcharge (Oct 15, 2026 –
  Jan 17, 2027) come off on top. Sell these as add-ons and bundles, or
  charge shipping at checkout — a single $1.81 ornament sold alone at $14.99
  with free shipping would barely break even after its $9.49 delivery.
- **Timing:** Printify production takes 2–7 business days, then US delivery
  takes another 2–5 business days — about a week order-to-door in practice.
  Printify's published 2026 Christmas cutoffs for US delivery by Dec 24 are
  **Dec 7 (Economy) to Dec 9 (Standard)** depending on provider. The store
  should advertise **"Order by December 7 for Christmas delivery."**

## The eight stocking stuffers

All production costs are Printify's published "from" prices (Free plan,
cheapest provider) as found Nov 20, 2026. Costs vary by provider — the
Shuttle should confirm the exact provider cost when each product is built.

### 1. Ceramic Ornament — the cheapest thing Printify makes
- **Production cost:** $1.81
- **Source:** Printify catalog page, Ceramic Ornament —
  https://printify.com/app/products/1628/generic-brand/ceramic-ornament
  (from $1.81; $1.31 with Printify Premium; shipping from $9.49)
- **Suggested retail:** $14.99
- **Margin:** $13.18 before shipping
- **Why it fits:** A Blackpine pine-tree ornament on the Christmas tree is
  the most on-brand stocking stuffer we can make. Best sold in bundles of
  2–3 or as an add-on to a larger order, because single-item shipping
  ($9.49+) eats the margin. Printify also sells this ornament in 1/5/10/20
  packs — a 3-pack bundle is the smart listing.

### 2. Kiss-Cut Sticker 3-Pack (3×3 in)
- **Production cost:** $1.20 each (domestic providers) to $2.88 each
  (Printify's official starting price) — so $3.60–$8.64 for a pack of 3
- **Sources:** PodVector's Printify guide (base $1.20–$2.80, shipping $3.99
  first item) —
  https://podvector.ai/articles/printify/products/the-complete-guide-to-printifys-most-profitable-products
  ; Printify's own profitable-products guide (stickers from $2.88) —
  https://printify.com/blog/most-profitable-print-on-demand-products/
- **Suggested retail:** $12.99 for the 3-pack
- **Margin:** $4.35–$9.39 per pack before shipping
- **Why it fits:** Stickers are the cheapest item to land at a customer's
  door (about $5.19 for one) and the classic stocking filler. Sell as a
  3-pack — a single sticker cannot carry a $10+ price on its own. Designs:
  faceted pine, "Roots Ran Deep," and a deer-track set.

## Timing and cutoff

- Production: 2–7 business days (Printify Help Center —
  https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/4483629751825-What-are-Printify-s-production-times-like),
  then 2–5 business days US delivery. Plan on about a week order-to-door.
- Printify's published 2026 Christmas cutoffs for US delivery by Dec 24:
  Dec 7 (Economy) to Dec 9 (Standard), varying by provider (compiled from
  Printify's network status page —
  https://bestprintondemand.co/holiday-deadlines).
- **Store cutoff to advertise: order by December 7 for Christmas delivery.**
  Orders after that may still arrive, but we cannot promise it.
