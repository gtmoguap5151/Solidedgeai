## Part 2 — The Mirror's click read: which reels actually drove store clicks?

**Short answer: the data that exists cannot answer this question, and The Mirror will not pretend otherwise.**

### What is on record

- **12 affiliate clicks EVER, 0 sales, $0.00.** That is the total, across the whole life of the store — not a per-reel or per-day figure.
- **Web Analytics is OFF** — Matt's decision. There are NO visitor numbers: no sessions, no page views, no referrers, no way to see when a visit happened or where it came from.
- **Nobody tracks which reel or post sent a click to the store.** There is no per-reel link, no per-post code, and no click log tied to a publish date. The 12 clicks are a single undated total from the affiliate side.

### What the 12 clicks can tell us

- Almost nothing about individual reels. The total predates and spans the countdown series, the earlier reels (blackpine-01/02/03, deer-season transition, deer checklist promo), the Facebook posts, and any other route to the store. With no dates attached to the clicks, they cannot be lined up against publish dates — a click could have come from any post at any time.
- What they do say, honestly: the store has had real but tiny affiliate interest (12 clicks), and none of it has converted (0 sales, $0.00). The bottleneck is not just traffic — it is traffic AND conversion, and at 12 clicks total there is not enough signal to judge either one by reel.

### What the 12 clicks cannot tell us

- Which reel — tn3day, tn2day, or any earlier one — drove any click.
- Which channel (Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Instagram) sent any click.
- Whether the countdown series is performing better or worse than the earlier reels.
- Whether any single post is worth repeating or retiring.

No view counts, click counts, or engagement numbers are stated here, because none are on record in the files or assignment for this draft. Any per-reel engagement that exists lives on the platforms themselves (views/likes/comments on each published reel) and would have to be read off each channel's own post — it is not in the store's numbers, and platform engagement still would not prove a store click.

### What CAN be known, with work that stays within the current rules

- **Publish dates vs. the click total, going forward:** if the affiliate click total is noted on the day before a reel publishes and again a day or two after, a jump in the total brackets that reel's window. It is coarse — it cannot separate one reel from another post in the same window, and it cannot name the channel — but it is the only attribution the current setup allows, and it costs nothing.
- **Per-channel engagement from the reels themselves:** likes, comments, shares, and view counts visible on each published reel (Facebook profile, TikTok, YouTube, Instagram) can be read and compared reel-to-reel. That measures attention, not store clicks — but attention is a real signal, and right now it is the only per-reel signal available. The Mirror can do this read once the numbers are pulled from the platforms; it has not been done yet, and no figures are invented here.

### Tracking proposal — what would actually answer the question

Without turning Web Analytics back on (that is Matt's call, not ours), the honest fix is to give each reel its own trackable path to the store:

1. **One distinct link per reel.** Give tn1day (and each reel after it) its own short link or tagged URL (e.g. a per-reel UTM tag on the blackpinefield.com link in the caption/bio). When a click arrives on that link, it belongs to that reel. This is the single change that turns "12 clicks ever" into "this reel drove this many."
2. **One link per channel, where the platform allows it.** The same reel on TikTok vs. YouTube vs. Facebook vs. Instagram should carry a channel-tagged variant, so the channel question gets answered too.
3. **A simple click log.** Note the affiliate click total (and any per-link counts, once per-reel links exist) on publish day and the day after, in one place, so the before/after comparison is a record instead of a memory.
4. **Keep it affiliate-side.** All of this uses the links and the affiliate dashboards that already exist — no analytics script on the store, no change to Matt's Web Analytics decision.

Until per-reel links exist, The Mirror's read stays what it is: 12 clicks ever, 0 sales, $0.00, unattributable to any reel. The countdown series may be working — but on the data that exists, nobody can say so, and nobody should claim to.
