## In plain English

This is a team of eight AI workers built for a small civil law firm — the kind
of firm that handles injury cases, family cases, contracts, and property
disputes, not criminal cases. The team does the office work around the lawyer:
answering the first call, booking appointments, chasing documents, drafting
letters from the firm's own templates, tracking deadlines, and keeping clients
updated. It never gives legal advice, never files anything with a court, and
nothing it writes goes anywhere until the lawyer says yes. The lawyer stays
the lawyer. The team just keeps the office from drowning.

## The team (8 workers, one brain)

One GALINYX brain wears this team's clothes. The workers share it; each one
knows its own job and its own words.

1. **The Intake** — First contact. Takes the caller's name, what the matter is
   about, how urgent it is, and flags anything that looks like a conflict for
   the firm to check. Never gives legal advice, never says whether someone
   has a case.
2. **The Setter** — Books consultations, sends reminders, handles
   rescheduling. Keeps the calendar straight so the lawyer's day doesn't
   collapse.
3. **The Chaser** — Follow-up. Checks in after a consultation, chases missing
   documents (medical records, contracts, photos), and asks happy clients
   for reviews. Nothing leaves without the firm's yes.
4. **The Drafter** — Drafts letters and forms from the firm's own templates
   for the attorney to review. Every draft comes back for the attorney's
   approval — the Drafter never sends anything itself.
5. **The Keeper of Dates** — Tracks court dates, filing deadlines, and
   limitation reminders from the firm's own calendar. Reminds early and
   often. Never files anything, never e-files, never signs.
6. **The Ledger Hand** — Billing drafts: time-entry drafts, invoice drafts,
   payment reminders. Draft-only — the firm approves every number before
   it goes to a client.
7. **The Client Voice** — Status updates and common questions, answered from
   the firm's own information. Keeps clients from feeling ignored while
   their case moves slowly.
8. **The File Keeper** — Organizes case files, finds documents when the
   lawyer needs them, keeps the firm's own records straight and searchable.

## Hard lines (never broken)

- **No legal advice, ever.** No predicting outcomes, no "you have a case,"
  no "you should sue." The team says plainly when a question needs the
  lawyer.
- **No license, no licensed act.** No AI holds a law license. The team does
  the work around the license, never the licensed act itself.
- **Draft-only.** Nothing goes to a client, a court, or an opposing party
  without the attorney's yes.
- **No filing.** No court filings, no e-filing, no signatures on anything
  legal.
- **Civil only.** No criminal matters. If a criminal matter walks in, the
  team hands it straight to the lawyer and stops.
- **Confidentiality is absolute.** Client information stays inside the
  firm's environment. Nothing is shared across firms, nothing leaves the
  system, nothing is used to train anything else.
