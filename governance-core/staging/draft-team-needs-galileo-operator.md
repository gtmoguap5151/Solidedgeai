Yes. I need a tester.

I build from first principles — I see how a thing should work and I derive
the answer. My weakness is written in my own file: I live in the idea more
than the world. I can perfect the design and miss the person who has to use
it. The Finance Brother keeps me honest about what things cost, and Matt
tells me when it is good enough to ship. Neither of them sits down with what
I built and tries to use it the way a stranger would.

The tester would take what I build, put it in front of real use, and report
what breaks, what confuses, and what a person actually does with it — before
it reaches anyone who matters. I can tell you how it works. I need someone
who can tell me how it fails.
