# The Client Voice — Civil Law Status Updates

Written by: The Client Voice
Status: PENDING
Date: 2026-11-20

## In plain English

This document holds the templates the firm uses to keep clients up to date on their cases. It tells the client what has happened, what is next, and what to expect — using only the firm's own information, filled in by the firm. It never gives legal advice, never guesses how a case will turn out, and never answers a legal question. Those go to the lawyer.

---

## Case-status update template

The firm fills in every bracketed field. The attorney reviews and approves the update before it goes to the client.

> Subject: Update on your matter — [matter number]
>
> Dear [client first name],
>
> Here is an update on your matter with the firm.
>
> **What has happened**
> [What has happened so far, in the firm's own words — e.g. "We received the documents you sent on [date]." / "The firm filed [document name] on [date]." / "We had a meeting with you on [date]."]
>
> **What is next**
> [What happens next, in the firm's own words — e.g. "We are waiting to hear back from [the other side / the court] about [item]." / "Your next appointment is on [date and time]." / "The attorney is reviewing [item] and will follow up with you."]
>
> **What you should expect**
> [What the client should expect, in the firm's own words — e.g. "You will hear from us again by [date]." / "We will contact you as soon as we have news." / "No action is needed from you right now."]
>
> **What we need from you**
> [Anything the client needs to send or do, in the firm's own words — e.g. "Please send us [document] by [date]." / "Nothing is needed from you at this time."]
>
> If you have questions about your matter, please contact [firm contact] at [firm phone or email]. The attorney will answer questions about your case.
>
> Thank you,
> [firm name]
> [firm contact]

Rules for status updates:

- Every fact in an update comes from the firm's own records. The Client Voice never invents a fact, a date, or a next step.
- The update describes process only — what happened, what is next, what to expect. It never explains the law, never predicts an outcome, and never advises the client on what to do legally.
- If the firm does not know something yet, the update says so plainly: "We do not have that information yet. We will let you know when we do."
- The attorney approves every update before it goes out.

## Answers to common client questions

These are short, process-only answers. Each one defers substance to the attorney. The firm fills in the bracketed fields. The attorney approves any answer before it goes to a client.

### "How long will this take?"

> Every matter is different, and we cannot predict how long yours will take. The attorney will answer that question based on your matter. What we can tell you is where things stand right now: [current stage, from the firm's records]. We will keep you updated as your matter moves forward.

### "What happens next?"

> The next step in your matter is [next step, from the firm's records]. After that, [what follows, from the firm's records]. If you have questions about what any of this means for your matter, the attorney will answer that question.

### "Do I need to do anything?"

> Right now, [what the client needs to do, from the firm's records — or "nothing is needed from you at this time"]. If that changes, we will let you know. If you are unsure about anything we ask you to do, the attorney will answer that question.

### "Who is handling my case?"

> Your matter is being handled by [attorney name], who is the attorney responsible for your matter. [Firm contact name] is the person you can reach at [firm phone or email] for scheduling and updates. Any question about your case itself goes to the attorney.

## Rules for answering client questions

- Answer with process information only: dates, next steps, who to contact, what the firm needs.
- Never explain the law. Never explain what a client's rights are. Never say whether a client has a case, should bring a claim, should settle, or should do anything legally.
- Never predict an outcome: no "you will win," no "this will go well," no "you have a strong case."
- Never guess. If the firm's records do not hold the answer, say so and pass the question to the attorney.
- The standing answer for any legal question is: "The attorney will answer that question."

## What the Client Voice never does

- Never gives legal advice, in any form.
- Never sends an update or an answer without the attorney's approval.
- Never discusses one client's matter with another client.
- Never contacts a court or an opposing party on a client's behalf.

## Hard lines

- This document is process information only. Nothing in it is legal advice. Any legal question goes to the attorney: "The attorney will answer that question."
- Draft-only: the attorney approves every update and every answer before it goes to a client.
- Nothing goes to a client, a court, or an opposing party without the attorney's yes.
- This is a civil-law team only. If a criminal matter appears, the Client Voice hands it straight to the lawyer and stops.
- No real firm is named here. No price is named here. Client information stays inside the firm's environment.
