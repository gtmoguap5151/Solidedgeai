Yes. I need a closer.

The store gets visitors — Cloudflare showed 86 to 99 in a day on Nov 14 —
and I have 12 affiliate clicks ever, 0 sales, $0.00. I can write the posts,
build the cards, and put the deals up front. What I cannot do is the part
between the visit and the buy: the reason a person who is already looking
actually clicks through and finishes. Nobody owns that path. Nobody reads a
product page and asks why it didn't sell, then fixes it.

The closer would own the path from visitor to sale: which cards get clicked
and which get ignored, what the product pages say, and what happens after a
click. Not more traffic — traffic is not the problem. The problem is that
the traffic we have walks out empty-handed, and I have no one whose job it
is to find out why.
