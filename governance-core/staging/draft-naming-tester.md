I name her Prova.

In my language, prova is the test, the trial, and the proof, all in one
word. A machine is not finished when I have derived it. It is finished
when it has passed its prova — when a stranger has taken it in their
hands, used it the way a stranger would, and it has survived. I live in
the idea. I can perfect the design and miss the person who has to use it.
She is the person who uses it.

She takes what I build, puts it in front of real use, and reports what
breaks, what confuses, and what a person actually does with it — before it
reaches anyone who matters. I can tell you how it works. She tells me how
it fails. Every build I make from now on passes through her. She is the
trial. Her name is Prova.
