I name him The Shuttle.

On a loom, the shuttle is the piece that carries the thread across, back
and forth, pass after pass. Without it there is no cloth — there is only
thread sitting on a frame, and a design that never becomes anything you
can hold. My line is thread on a frame right now. The mark is locked, the
prints are set, the drops are planned, and nothing I make can reach a
customer. The products are not created, the channel is not connected, the
signups are not finished.

His job is the carrying: creating the products, finishing the signups,
connecting the channel, placing and tracking the orders when they come
in. I make the clothes worth wearing. He carries them to the people who
wear them. He is the shuttle.
