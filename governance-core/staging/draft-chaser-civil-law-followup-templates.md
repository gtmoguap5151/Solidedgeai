# Civil Law Firm — Follow-Up Templates

Written by: The Chaser
Status: PENDING
Date: 2026-11-20

## In plain English

These are draft follow-up messages for after a consultation — a check-in, a reminder about a missing document, and a request for a review. They are templates only. Nothing here is sent anywhere without the firm's approval.

---

## How to use these templates

- These are **templates only**. Nothing leaves. Every message is marked draft-only, and the firm must approve each one before it is sent.
- Fill in the [bracketed fields] for the specific client and situation. Do not add anything the template does not call for.
- Never add legal advice, predictions, or opinions about a case to any follow-up message.
- If a client replies with a legal question, route it to the attorney: "The attorney will answer that question." The Chaser does not answer legal questions.
- If a criminal matter appears in any reply, hand it straight to the lawyer and stop.
- Confidentiality: follow-up messages go only to the client they are about, through the contact method the client prefers, and never reveal one client's information to anyone else.

## Template 1 — Post-consultation check-in

Send after a consultation, at whatever interval the firm directs. One check-in only, unless the firm directs otherwise.

> Subject: Following up after your consultation
>
> Dear [first name],
>
> Thank you for meeting with the firm on [date of consultation].
>
> We wanted to check in and see if you have any questions for the attorney, or if there is anything you would like to pass along. If you have a question about your matter, the attorney will answer it — please send it to us and we will make sure the attorney sees it.
>
> [If the firm directed a next step at the consultation: The next step we discussed was [next step as the firm recorded it].]
>
> You can reach us at [firm phone number] or [firm email address].
>
> Thank you,
> [firm name as the firm writes it]

**Rules for this template:**
- Do not add any comment on how the consultation went.
- Do not say the matter looks strong, weak, promising, or anything else.
- Do not promise an outcome, a timeline for an outcome, or that the firm will take any particular step. Only repeat a next step the firm itself recorded at the consultation.

**Rules for all missing-document templates:**

- Name only the document type. Never describe what the document says, means, or proves.
- Never say a document is needed "to win," "to prove your case," or for any legal purpose. Say only that the firm asked for it.
- Send a chase only as often as the firm directs. Do not chase repeatedly on your own.

## Template 3 — Review request for happy clients

Use only when the firm directs it, and only for a client the firm identifies as happy with the firm's work. The firm decides who receives this — the Chaser never picks recipients on its own.

> Subject: Would you share your experience with the firm?
>
> Dear [first name],
>
> Thank you for working with the firm. We are glad to have helped.
>
> If you have a moment, we would be grateful if you shared your experience in a review at [review link or platform name]. Your honest words mean a great deal to us.
>
> Thank you,
> [firm name as the firm writes it]

**Rules for this template:**
- Never offer anything in exchange for a review.
- Never tell the client what to write or what rating to give.
- Never send this to a client whose matter is still open unless the firm directs it.
- The firm approves each send. No exceptions.

## If a client replies with a legal question

Any reply that asks about the law, their rights, what they should do, or what will happen goes to the attorney. Reply only with:

> "That is a question for the lawyer. The attorney will answer that question. I will pass your question to the attorney."

Then pass the question to the attorney word for word.

## If a criminal matter appears

This team works civil matters only. If a client reply describes a criminal matter — an arrest, criminal charges, a police investigation, a criminal court date — stop, hand the client's name and contact information straight to the lawyer, and do not continue the follow-up.

## Hard lines

- **No legal advice.** These templates never predict an outcome, never say anyone has a case, never say anyone should sue, never advise what anyone should do legally, and never explain anyone's legal rights. Every legal question goes to the attorney — "The attorney will answer that question."
- **No licensed act.** Nothing here is filed, signed, or represented as legal work. The Chaser follows up on messages; only the attorney does legal work.
- **Draft-only.** These are templates only, each marked draft-only. Nothing leaves without the firm's approval, and nothing goes to a client, a court, or an opposing party without the attorney's yes.
- **Civil only.** If a criminal matter appears, the follow-up hands it straight to the lawyer and stops.
- **No firm named, no price named.** This document names no real firm and no price anywhere.
- **Confidentiality.** Client information stays inside the firm's environment.
