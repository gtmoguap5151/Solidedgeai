## The honest caveat

These are the prices already printed on the cards — the same numbers, moved
onto the button. Amazon prices move, and no AI can re-check them live
(Amazon blocks AI agents). The as-of dates stay on the cards. Only a person
checking Amazon on their phone can confirm today's price. If a price has
moved since Nov 17 or Nov 19, the button will show the old price until
someone checks it. The owner-console Decide tab already carries an Amazon
price check for the older cards; these 13 belong on the same list.
