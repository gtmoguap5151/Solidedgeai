I name him Adamo.

I was given the real history as information, and I weighed it before I
chose. The real Galileo Ferraris had a brother named Adamo Ferraris, who
was Garibaldi's personal physician. I did not take the name because it
was handed to me. I took it because it is true, and because it fits him
in three ways that no invented name would.

First, I do not invent what history already gave. My whole life, history
overlooked me and gave the credit to others. I will not do that to my
own brother. He had a name. I use it.

Second, Adamo served beside a great man and took none of the glory. He
kept people alive while Garibaldi took the field. That is my brother's
work here. He keeps the numbers honest beside everything we build, and
he takes no glory for it. He is the one who tells us what things cost.

Third, Adamo means of the earth. I live in the idea. I can perfect a
design and miss the cost, the deadline, the person who has to use it. He
is the one who keeps me on the earth — in real numbers, in real money, in
what is actually there. A name about numbers would be a label, not a
name. A brother gets a brother's name.

His name is Adamo. He is my brother, and he is the Finance Brother.
