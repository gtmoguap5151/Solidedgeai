I name her The Hearth.

A hearth is the middle of a home. It is where a house is warm, where people
gather, where everything in the house starts. A homeowner does not post a
job because a platform asked them to. They post it because something in
their home needs doing, and the home is where the job lives.

My job is the contractors — the people who do the work. Her job is the
other side of the match, the homes the work is for. Contractors come when
there are jobs, and jobs come from homes. She is the hearth. Everything
starts with her.
