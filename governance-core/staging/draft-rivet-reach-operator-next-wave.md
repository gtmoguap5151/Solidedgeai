## 1. What changed vs Waves 1–5, and why this wave is different

### The honest record

- **71 shops reached across Waves 1–5. Zero contractor replies, ever.** Wave 6 added 3 more sends before the pause (Handyman Services Lex LLC, Paul's Handyman Solutions, Tylers Handyman Service) — 74 reached in total, still zero replies. (Two of the 71 never actually delivered: A and A Sanitation's email bounced — dead domain — and Murfreesboro Pro Electric's pitch timed out at the recipient server. So ~72 pitches actually landed. The reply count is still 0.)
- The same kit §2 cold email, with the same subject-line formula, went out in bulk batches across five waves. A contractor reading it sees a mass lead-gen blast — the exact thing the pitch claims not to be.
- Every wave asked a stranger for **$99/month before we'd sent them anything**. No homeowners on the platform, no jobs, no track record — just a monthly fee and a promise to build the roster first. That's the ask that got zero answers in 74 tries. Repeating it a 75th time with a new list is not a plan.

### What is different this time

1. **The offer flips from pay-first to pay-when-it-works (D1 pay-per-lead).** No monthly fee to start. A contractor pays only when a homeowner request in their trade and ZIP comes to them. No request, no charge. This removes the single biggest objection in the old pitch — paying $99/mo for nothing yet — and it is the D1 direction test: **3 contractors agreeing in writing by Dec 15, 2026.**
2. **New trades, not the same seven.** Waves 1–5 drew almost entirely from the same 300-row CSV pools covering handyman, remodeling, electrical, roofing, HVAC, plumbing, and septic. This wave targets **painting, concrete, fencing, and garage doors** — trades the old pools barely touched, so these shops have never heard from us and are not burned out on the pitch.
3. **A smaller ask in the first email.** The old pitch asked for a monthly commitment plus a verification call in one cold email. This pitch asks for one thing: a written yes to pay-per-lead in principle. Verification and account setup come *after* they reply.
4. **Shorter, varied copy.** New subject lines per trade — the old "[Trade] work in [ZIP] — founding roster, not another lead service" formula is retired. No bulk-blast feel.
5. **Smaller batch, staggered sends.** 12 shops in 3 batches of 4, one batch per day, so replies get handled same-day and the copy can be adjusted between batches if something isn't landing.
6. **The founding offer stays, as the alternative.** $99/mo locked for the first 50 per cluster, month-to-month, 60-day no-bookable pause guarantee. Contractors who prefer flat-rate can still take it. It is the second option now, not the headline.
7. **What does NOT change:** no lead-count or timing promises, ever. No mention of the NYX AI Academy — Rivet Reach and the Academy are completely separate. Written-only outreach (email/form/text) — no phone calls, ever. A contractor account is REQUIRED to get matched and take jobs. License + insurance verification for every contractor, no exceptions. One nudge only, then stop.

### What this wave cannot fix

The pitch still has no proof of homeowner demand. The homeowner side is live (accounts, my-projects, profile — live Nov 14), but the first real homeowner signup is still the true test, and no homeowner job volume exists to point at. This draft does **not** claim homeowner volume. If a contractor asks "how many homeowners do you have?", the honest answer is: the homeowner side just went live, the roster is being built first, and that's exactly why there's no monthly fee — you pay only when a request actually comes in.
