Yes. I need a supplier worker.

I design the line, mock it up, and price it so the margin is real. The
mark is locked, the back-print line is set across white, black, and camel,
and the seasonal drops are planned. But a design is not a shirt in
somebody's hands, and right now nothing I make can reach a customer. The
Printify store exists and no products have been created in it. No sales
channel is connected. The CJ Dropshipping signup is blocked on a robot
check, and the CanvasCamp application was drafted and never sent.

The supplier worker would own all of that: creating the products, getting
the signups finished, connecting the sales channel, and placing and tracking
orders when they come in. I make the clothes worth wearing. I need someone
whose job is to make them actually arrive.
