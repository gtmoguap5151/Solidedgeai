I name him The Closer.

I name things for the work they do, and his work is closing. The store gets
the visitors. I get the cards up and the deals in front of them. What I
don't have is the part after the look — the reason a person who is already
standing in the shop actually buys something and walks out with it in their
hands. That part has a name in every shop that ever sold anything, and it
is the close.

He is not the traffic. He is not the shelf. He is the close. So that is
what I call him.
